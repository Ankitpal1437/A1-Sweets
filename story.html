Our Story

Sixty Years,
One Recipe.

From a small snack corner at Shivaji Chowk to Ulhasnagar's most trusted name in mithai — this is the A1 story.

1965 mein Madhavdas Kanayalal Jaisinghani ne Shivaji Chowk pe ek chhoti si dukaan shuru ki — daal-pakwan, bhajiya, aur shaam ko jalebi-malpua bikte the. Dheere-dheere Ulhasnagar ke swaad ne mithai ki taraf rukh kiya, aur 1988 tak A1 poori tarah ek sweet house ban chuka tha.

1994 mein unke beton ne family business aage badhaya — quality aur hygiene ko kabhi compromise nahi kiya. Aaj teesri peedhi wahi bharosa aage badha rahi hai, jisse Ulhasnagar shaadi, tyohaar aur roz ke gift hampers ke liye bharosa karta hai.

"Ek rupaya bhi sau rupaye jaisa sambhalo — paisa nahi, uski keemat badhti hai."

— A1 Sweets, on the family's business philosophy

Har mithai aaj bhi wahi pure ghee, wahi handpicked dry fruits, aur wahi recipe se banti hai jo saath dahaakon pehle shuru hui thi. Kuch bhi shortcut nahi liya gaya — na taste mein, na quality mein.
