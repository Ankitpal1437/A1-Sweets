01The Story

Sixty years,
one recipe.

Ek rupaya bhi sau rupaye jaisa sambhalo — paisa nahi, uski keemat badhti hai.

1965 mein Madhavdas Kanayalal Jaisinghani ne Shivaji Chowk pe ek chhoti si dukaan shuru ki. Dheere-dheere Ulhasnagar ke swaad ne mithai ki taraf rukh kiya — aur 1988 tak A1 poori tarah ek sweet house ban chuka tha. Aaj teesri peedhi wahi bharosa aage badha rahi hai.
