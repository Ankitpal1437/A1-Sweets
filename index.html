Our Story

Ek snack corner se,
ek legacy tak.

1965 mein Madhavdas Kanayalal Jaisinghani ne Shivaji Chowk pe ek chhoti si dukaan shuru ki. Dheere-dheere Ulhasnagar ke swaad ne mithai ki taraf rukh kiya — aur 1988 tak A1 poori tarah ek sweet house ban chuka tha.

Aaj teesri peedhi isse aage badha rahi hai — wahi recipe, wahi ghee, wahi bharosa jo shuru se raha hai.
